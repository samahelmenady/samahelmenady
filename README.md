I'm a B.Sc. Artificial Intelligence student at New Mansoura University (2023 – 2027) focused on **Generative AI**, with hands-on experience in **LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, machine learning, deep learning, and NLP**.

I design, build, and deploy AI applications using **Python, LangChain, Google Cloud Platform, and Vertex AI**, with an emphasis on scalable systems that solve real problems through automation and intelligent decision-making.
